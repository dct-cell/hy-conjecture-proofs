# Problem 44: A surface with no one-fibered primary ideals

[Problem](problem.md) · [HY result](hy_result.md) · [Original paper](https://www.mat.uniroma3.it/users/fontana/documents/CahenFontanaFrischGlaz_OpenProblemsArticle%20%282%29.pdf)

A specified geometric construction of a two-dimensional complete normal Noetherian local domain with at least two Rees valuation rings for every maximal-primary ideal. It specializes Cutkosky's geometric construction; it does not supply explicit power-series quotient equations. See also [Searching for Cutkosky's example](https://www.math.purdue.edu/~iswanso/DGS.pdf).

Text review: complete.

Lean: Partial local formalization. The complete ring construction and its
all-primary-ideals conclusion are not yet verified; see the
[scope and audit report](../reports/2026-11-09-37c-44.md).
