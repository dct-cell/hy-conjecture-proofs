## Problem 43: the proved scope

The closed theorem `Problem43.AlgebraCore.algebraicCertificate` proves an explicitly named **partial** specification. The actual rings are $R=\mathbb Q[u,ut^2,ut]$, $S=\mathbb Q[u,t^2,ut]$, $B=\mathbb Q[u,t]$ and $F=\operatorname{Frac}(B)$.

It covers the Noetherian domain properties and inclusions; finite type and torsion-freeness; the common fraction field and $F\otimes_R S\cong F$; normality of $R$ and every prime localization of $R$; the actual cone and relative quotient presentations; the finite normalization $B$ of $S$; and the derivative-ideal computations and Jacobian-colon obstruction in $F$.

The base-ring normality proof is supplied, rather than taken as an additional hypothesis. It uses an isomorphism to a fixed subalgebra of a polynomial ring and a proved normality lemma for fixed subalgebras.

## Problem 43: outstanding obligations

The following are **not** proved by that certificate:

- Openness of the $S_2$-locus for every essentially finite type $R$-algebra.
- The relatively $S_2$ presentation at every maximal ideal, with all height and depth conditions.
- The required height-one prime and its contraction to the cone vertex, together with nonregularity of the specified local ring.
- The formal comparison of the weaker hypothesis with the original regularity hypothesis and assembly of the complete original target.

Consequently, the algebraic certificate is not a complete formal verification of the submitted counterexample. These are formalization gaps; this report does not infer a defect in the written mathematical proof from them.
